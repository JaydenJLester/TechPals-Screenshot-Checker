Good screenshot practices

A complete, readable screenshot gives the checker better information.

Include

  • The full message or pop-up
  • The sender name or email address
  • Visible links, buttons, and warnings

Avoid

  • Blurry or cut-off text
  • Several screenshots in one image
  • Unrelated personal information

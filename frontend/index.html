Helpful Tips

Good screenshots include:

  • The full message, email, pop-up, or notification.
  • The sender name or email address, if visible.
  • Any links, buttons, warnings, or instructions.
  • Clear text that is not too blurry or cut off.

Try not to upload:

  • Images that are too blurry to read.
  • Screenshots with most of the message cut off.
  • Multiple screenshots at once.
